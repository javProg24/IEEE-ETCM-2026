GETTING THERE

✈️ BY AIR

José Joaquín de Olmedo International Airport (GYE) is Guayaquil's main airport, located approximately 15–20 minutes from ITB campus.

Direct flights from Quito, Bogotá, Lima, Panama City, Miami, Madrid, and other major hubs.

🚕 TAXI & RIDE SHARE

Taxis and Uber are widely available outside arrivals. Estimated fare to ITB: USD 8–12 depending on traffic.

🏙️ CITY CENTRE

ITB is located in downtown Guayaquil, easily accessible from major city landmarks and transportation hubs.

Travel tip: Ecuador uses the US Dollar (USD) as its official currency. No currency exchange needed for travelers arriving from the United States.
