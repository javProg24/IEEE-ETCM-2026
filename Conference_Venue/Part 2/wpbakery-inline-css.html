Conference Venue Location

Instituto Superior Universitario Bolivariano de Tecnologia

The official conference venue of IEEE ETCM 2026. One of Ecuador's most acclaimed educational institutions, combining world-class conference facilities with exceptional hospitality in the heart of Guayaquil.

The Instituto Superior Universitario Bolivariano de Tecnología (ITB) is a leading technical university in Ecuador. It provides state-of-the-art facilities for academic conferences and technical meetings. The campus features modern auditoriums, advanced audio-visual equipment, and comfortable spaces designed for networking and collaboration.

Getting to Guayaquil

Guayaquil is Ecuador's largest city and main economic hub. The city is well-connected internationally through José Joaquín de Olmedo International Airport. Whether you're traveling from North America, South America, or Europe, reaching Guayaquil is convenient with multiple daily flights from major cities.

Transportation Options to the Conference Venue

✈️ BY AIR

José Joaquín de Olmedo International Airport (GYE) is Guayaquil's main airport. It is located approximately 15–20 minutes from the ITB campus by car.

Direct flights available from Quito, Bogotá, Lima, Panama City, Miami, Madrid, and other major international hubs.

🚕 TAXI & RIDE SHARE

Taxis and Uber are widely available outside the airport arrivals terminal. Estimated fare to ITB: USD 8–12 depending on traffic conditions.

We recommend using official airport taxis or ride-sharing apps for safety and convenience.

🏙️ CITY CENTRE

ITB is located in downtown Guayaquil, making it easily accessible from major city landmarks, hotels, and transportation hubs.

Walking distance to Malecón 2000, restaurants, and cultural attractions.

Travel tip: Ecuador uses the US Dollar (USD) as its official currency. No currency exchange is needed for travelers arriving from the United States. ATMs are widely available throughout Guayaquil.

Additional Travel Information

International attendees should check visa requirements for Ecuador before traveling. Many countries have visa-free access for tourism and business purposes. For more information, visit the Ecuador Ministry of Foreign Affairs website.

Accommodation & Venue Facilities

The IEEE ETCM 2026 conference will be held entirely at the ITB campus facilities. We recommend booking accommodation at nearby hotels in downtown Guayaquil for convenient access to the venue and local attractions.
