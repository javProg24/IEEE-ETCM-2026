Getting There

✈️ BY AIR

José Joaquín de Olmedo International Airport (GYE) is Guayaquil's main airport, located 15–20 minutes from the hotel.

Direct flights from Quito, Bogotá, Lima, Panama City, Miami, Madrid, and other major hubs.

🚕 TAXI & RIDE SHARE

Taxis and Uber are widely available outside arrivals. Estimated fare to the hotel: USD 8–12 depending on traffic.

The hotel also offers an airport shuttle service — contact the hotel in advance to arrange a pickup.

🏙️ CITY CENTRE

Hotel Oro Verde is in the heart of downtown Guayaquil, steps from the Malecón 2000 waterfront promenade and the Las Peñas cultural district.

Valet parking available on-site for attendees arriving by car.

Travel tip: Ecuador uses the US Dollar (USD) as its official currency. No currency exchange needed for travelers arriving from the United States.
