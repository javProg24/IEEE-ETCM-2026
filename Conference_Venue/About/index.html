Guayaquil, Ecuador’s largest city and principal economic hub, offers a dynamic environment for international academic and professional events. With a metropolitan population of more than 2.7 million people, it serves as a strategic center for commerce, industry, and global connectivity.

Located on the banks of the Guayas River near the Pacific Ocean, the city blends modern infrastructure with coastal identity. Its convention services, hotel offer, and international accessibility make Guayaquil an attractive destination for conferences and institutional gatherings.

Among its most emblematic attractions is Malecón 2000, a revitalized waterfront promenade with gardens, cultural spaces, restaurants, and panoramic river views, reflecting the city’s energy, hospitality, and urban transformation.
