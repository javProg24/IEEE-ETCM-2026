ABOUT ITB

The Instituto Tecnologico Bolivariano de Tecnologia (ITB) is a higher education institution located in Guayaquil, Ecuador. It is recognized for offering technical and technological education aimed at preparing students for the labor market through practical and innovative training.

ITB provides a variety of academic programs in areas such as business administration, marketing, information technology, gastronomy, tourism, and health-related fields. The institute focuses on developing professional skills, entrepreneurship, and technological competencies that respond to the needs of modern industries.

Through its educational model, ITB promotes values such as responsibility, innovation, and commitment to professional excellence. Its programs emphasize practical learning, allowing students to acquire real-world experience and strengthen their professional development. The institute has become an important educational option in Guayaquil for students seeking quality technological education and opportunities for career growth.

The university follows the Ignatian Pedagogical Paradigm, promoting integral education rooted in values such as justice, service, and faith. It has several campuses throughout Ecuador, expanding access to higher education.
