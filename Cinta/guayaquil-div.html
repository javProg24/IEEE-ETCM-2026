City Profile

Guayaquil

Guayaquil is Ecuador's largest city and one of the country's main economic, commercial, and port hubs. Set along the Guayas River, it combines waterfront renewal, urban energy, and strong regional identity.

Now showing
Major Port City
A strategic logistics and trade center connected to Ecuador's coastal economy.

Location

Located in western Ecuador, on the banks of the Guayas River near the Pacific corridor.

Character

Known for entrepreneurship, port activity, large-scale commerce, and fast-paced urban growth.

Landmarks

Malecon 2000, Las Penas, Santa Ana Hill, and La Rotonda are among its best-known attractions.

Climate

Tropical and warm, with humid conditions shaping much of the city's atmosphere year-round.
